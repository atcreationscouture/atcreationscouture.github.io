Craftsmanship Disciplines

✦

Every custom garment is envisioned as an heirloom. We combine traditional Maggam frame handwork with sharp multi-thread machine design.

Generational Heritage

Handcrafted Aari & Zardozi

Crafted meticulously on traditional wooden Maggam stretch frames using pointed hook needles. Richly enriched with genuine French wire zardozi, Austrian cutdana beads, seed pearls (moti), and Kundan stone framing.

  • Antique Zari and Metallic Wire
  • Traditional Temple & Floral Motifs
  • Layered 3D Relief Stitching
Contemporary Symmetry

Machine Embroidery

High-density multi-thread computerized needlework designed for crisp architectural geometric lines, lightweight occasion wear, and repetitive border elegance with uniform satin stitch luster.

  • Architectural Jaal & Geometric Grids
  • Multi-Color Resham Silk Threads
  • Lightweight & Durable Occasion Wear
Customized Storytelling

Bespoke Customization

Tailor-made commissions starting completely from your inspiration photos, wedding theme, and fabric swatches. We incorporate personalized wedding dates, couple monograms, and custom neckline cuts.

  • Personalized Monogram & Date Patches
  • Pure Kanchipuram Brocade Matching
  • Coordinated Trousseau & Keepsakes
The Commission Process

How We Craft Your Heirloom

Four deliberate steps to transform your fabric and design vision into an exquisite work of embroidery.

01

Design Consultation

Share your saree color, event date, and design ideas via WhatsApp or visit our Mannivakkam studio.

02

Motif & Tracing

We draft precise necklines, sleeve dimensions, and paper tracings tailored to your exact measurements.

03

Master Needlework

Artisans stitch thread by thread using pure zari, cutbeads, and zardozi on dedicated timber frames.

04

Fitting & Delivery

Final inspection, custom tailoring if requested, and secure insured delivery right to your doorstep.

Atelier Kavya Shyamsundar

"Every stitch is an intimate prayer of devotion to Indian needlecraft."

I founded AT Creations with an unwavering reverence for the time-honored Maggam frames of Tamil Nadu and the storied needlework traditions of Indian wedding trousseaus. In an era of mass-produced uniformity, we believe that bridal wear should be as unique and personal as the vows you speak.

Our boutique studio at Ruby Royal Tower, Mannivakkam was created to offer brides a genuine creative sanctuary. Whether you bring an ancestral Kanjivaram saree passed down through generations or wish to design a fresh occasion silhouette, each needle pull, micro-pearl, and zardozi spiral is placed with heartfelt intention.
