The Craft Matrix

✦

We treat needlecraft not as embellishment, but as sculpture on cloth. Each commission unites classical Maggam timber frames with fine computerized thread arrays.

Discipline I

Handcrafted Aari & Zardozi

Stitched slowly on heavy wooden Maggam tension frames using micro-awl hook needles. We weave French wire zardozi coils, real Austrian cutdana beads, rice pearls (moti), and raw Kundan stones.

  • Antique Gold & Metallic Dabka Wire
  • Sacred Temple Gopuram & Floral Arches
  • 3D Padded Relief Stitching (Gaddi Work)
Discipline II

Machine Embroidery

High-density computerized needlework calibrated for flawless geometric repetition, crisp architectural jaals, and lightweight occasion wear with vibrant multi-hued resham silk threads.

  • Architectural Jaal & Geometric Trellises
  • Multi-Shade Resham Silk Shading
  • Crisp, Even Borders for Occasion Sarees
Discipline III

Bespoke Couture Fusion

Tailored specifically from your bridal color palette, saree drape, and silhouette preferences. Incorporates custom couple monograms, auspicious dates, and neckline sculpting.

  • Hand-Rendered Couple Monogram Crests
  • Kanchipuram Brocade Color Harmonization
  • Coordinated Trousseau & Family Blouses
Provenance & Purity

Material & Thread Archive

We use strictly non-tarnishing metallic wires, certified raw mulberry silks, and jewel-grade crystals.

01

French Wire Dabka

Coiled electroplated copper wire that reflects light with an antique candlelight sheen. Resists oxidative discoloration.

02

Austrian Cutbeads

Precision micro-cylinders faceted to catch ambient banquet lights without adding cumbersome weight to your blouse sleeves.

03

Pure Mulberry Silk

High-GSM raw and dupion silks that endure dense maggam embroidery needle tension without tearing or sagging.

04

Basra Seed Pearls (Moti)

Hand-strung micro seed pearls used to frame zardozi medallions and neckline contours in classical South Indian bridal grammar.

Atelier Kavya Shyamsundar

"Every stitch is an intimate prayer of devotion to Indian needlecraft."

I founded AT Creations with an unwavering reverence for the time-honored Maggam frames of Tamil Nadu and the storied needlework traditions of Indian wedding trousseaus. In an era of mass-produced uniformity, we believe that bridal wear should be as unique and personal as the vows you speak.

Our boutique studio at Ruby Royal Tower, Mannivakkam was created to offer brides a genuine creative sanctuary. Whether you bring an ancestral Kanjivaram saree passed down through generations or wish to design a fresh occasion silhouette, each needle pull, micro-pearl, and zardozi spiral is placed with heartfelt intention.
